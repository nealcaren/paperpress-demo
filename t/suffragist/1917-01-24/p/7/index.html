Down all the history of democracy people have established their rights by showing thejr strength to the men in power. It is no new move for the disfranchised to march upon parliaments and surround heads of governments to remind them that they are waiting for justice. Let women, then, join in a deputation of such size that it will move the President and the Democratic party to recognize the strength as they have recognized the justice of their demand.
