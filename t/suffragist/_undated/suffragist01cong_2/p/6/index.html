In the course of the hearing Mr. Henry asked Dr. Shaw if the National Association would be satisfied if the Committee on the Election of President, Vice-President, and Representatives in Congress were given jurisdiction over the question of Woman Suffrage also. Dr. Shaw, after consultation with the National Board, said emphatically “No." The Committee on the Election of President, Vice-president, and Representatives in Congress, Dr. Shaw stated, would also be a very busy one in the coming session, since the question of presidential primaries urged by President Wilson in his message to Congress would come in due course to this committee. Suffragists would gair no jot of advantage by the trans ference of their question from one busy committee to another.
